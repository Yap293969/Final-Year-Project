FYP2 — Academic research prototype

Detecting phishing,
one link at a time

Is this a scam? pairs BERT, which reads text in context, with an XGBoost classifier and 35 hand-built URL features — turning any email or link into one explainable verdict, with SHAP showing the reasons behind it.

01 How it works

  1. 01

    Paste

    An email’s subject and message, or a web address you’re unsure about.

  2. 02

    Read

    BERT turns the text into 768 numbers that capture what it means in context.

  3. 03

    Decide

    XGBoost weighs those numbers — plus 35 URL features for links — and gives a phishing probability.

  4. 04

    Explain

    SHAP shows which words or features pushed the answer towards phishing or legitimate.

A research prototype built for a Final Year Project, not a security product. It can be wrong in both directions — never rely on it alone to decide whether something is safe.
